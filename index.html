BlockPilot Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · White Thang Studio

BlockPilot is an RV leveling app for Android. It works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app does not have internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone, apart from Android's own backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on.

Motion sensors

BlockPilot reads your phone's motion sensors to measure roll and pitch. These readings are used on the device to calculate how many blocks you need. They are not stored or shared. Calibration offsets you choose to save are kept with your vehicle profiles.

Location

BlockPilot does not access your location.

Information you enter

Vehicle profiles, block kits, calibrations and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Backups

BlockPilot uses Android's built-in backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android includes your vehicle profiles, block kits, calibrations and settings in the encrypted backup stored in your own Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling. Backups are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot access them. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling BlockPilot, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of its data from your phone. Backups in your Google account are managed from your Google account and Android settings.

Children

BlockPilot is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
